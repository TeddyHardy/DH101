## Reflection

My GIF shows someone contemplating driving somewhere. The GIF starts with them in their bed, getting up and going to their car, but then back to their bed. It then repeats.

If this was an image rather than a GIF, it would represent someone about to leave. By looping my GIF, the meaning changes. The person never goes anywhere other than from their bed to the car, then back to bed. This represents someone hesitating and being stuck, rather than just an image of someone about to drive their car. Before my GIF repeats, you would expect them to drive off. By the third loop, you stop waiting for that person to leave and know that they will eventually get back in bed. Including repetition in my GIF turns an ordinary morning into something more than that. It is funny at first, but after a while it gets repetitive which can be frustrating. My GIF shows what procrastination and low energy feel like, which was the meaning behind my GIF.

I see the human choice in my GIF as I told the AI tool what I wanted the sequence of events to be. AI comes into play as it created the scene, what the character looked like, and how long it takes for the character to decide to go back inside. AI complicates the ideas of authorship as it never asked me what I wanted the character to look like. It decided the characters features, the house its staged in, the car, and much more on its own without asking me for my thoughts.
