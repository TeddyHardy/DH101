## Commentary 

This comic assignment helped me understand how AI can be very helpful, but it also has its complications. I asked ChatGPT’s image generator to create a comic of “T-Rex Teddy” saving New York City. The AI generator was easily able to make each panel, but it was not the story I was trying to tell. At first, it added its own villain, which I had not told it to do. I had to then describe the villain I was picturing and which panel that I wanted it in. That is where the confusion between creating your own original comic and AI’s version of a comic kicks in. I realized that if I wanted my comic to turn out how I pictured it I would need to go panel by panel and describe each scene.

Using this panel-by-panel format, I was able to see much more of what I wanted rather than having AI do the whole thing itself. Much more of the detail I wanted was added after I changed my strategy, and I was now able to see the benefits of using AI to create my comic.

This project made me realize that AI cannot generate its own comic or story on its own, it needs you to describe what you want for a better result. The plot of the story cannot be decided without a human telling the AI what to do. If I was not there to intervene, the story would have turned out completely different.
