## Reflection 1

**Where do I see myself in the image – and where do you not?**

In the first AI generated image, I can see clear differences between the original and the fake image. My skin is much clearer, and I am now dressed professionally. This is exactly what I told the AI generator to do, so I was not surprised by these results. I am not slumped down which I was in the original image; the generated image makes me look much more engaged and present.

In the second AI generated image, I am walking down a tunnel, also in a suit. There are much clearer differences between this image that the original image I uploaded, as I look serious, confident, taller, and professional. There are now cameras in the background, something that was clearly not in my uploaded image. The differences are noticeable, but the AI generated image still looks real.

I found it interesting how you can take an image of yourself in your classroom, and minutes later can have an image that looks much more professional in a completely different setting. As AI becomes more advanced, I think the noticeable differences between a real image and an AI image will disappear. This makes me wonder how people will be able to tell what is real once AI images become even more realistic.

## Reflection 2

**What does “authenticity” mean when identity is co-constructed with AI?**

When identity is co-constructed with AI, authenticity changes from matching reality to creating something with intention. Identity when constructed by AI isn’t real but can still be authentic if it reflects what you want it to reflect.

## Attribution & AI Use

**Tools Used:** Chat GPT, GPT Image

**Prompts:**

1.  Touch up my skin and hair and make it look like I am a model.

2.  Now take the generated image and put me in a stadium tunnel, wearing headphones and carrying a briefcase like I am a professional athlete arriving to a big game with cameramen in the background taking pictures of me. Make me look serious like I am focused on the game.

**What the AI generated:**

First Image: It generated a photo of me in a suit, sitting in the same position as the photo I uploaded

Second Image: It took the generated image of me and put me in a stadium tunnel, holding a briefcase and wearing headphones, as if I was an athlete arriving to a game.

**What I changed or decided:**

In the first AI generated image, I had it tone my skin and fix my hair. In the second AI generated image, I had it relocate me to a stadium and make me look even more professional.
