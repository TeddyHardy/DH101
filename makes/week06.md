## ChatGPT

I asked for a rhetorical analysis on this short story. Chat GPT’s response was:

> “Poe uses **ethos, pathos, logos, repetition, and imagery** in *The Tell-Tale Heart* to show the narrator’s unstable mental state. The narrator uses **ethos** by repeatedly claiming that he is sane, saying, “How, then, am I mad?” However, his arguments actually make him seem less trustworthy.

>
> Poe also uses **pathos** by creating fear and tension. The narrator describes the old man’s terror and his own growing anxiety. Poe uses **repetition** when the narrator hears the heartbeat becoming “louder, louder,” showing his increasing panic. 

>
> Overall, Poe’s rhetorical choices make the reader feel the narrator’s fear and confusion while showing that his attempt to prove he is sane actually proves the opposite.”

## Reflection

**What do we gain and what do we lose when machines “read” literature for us? How do Voyant and GPT imagine “meaning” differently?**

Machines give us speed and proof. Voyant tools counted every word in the short story in seconds and showed what words Poe kept using. I could see the repetition while I was reading, but Voyant backed it up with numbers. On the other hand, Chat GPT explained how the narrator’s arguments for his sanity proved the opposite. What Voyant tells me is the most common words, but not why it matters. Chat GPT explains the meaning of the short story but does not use numbers as evidence.

Voyant treats meaning as something measured. It believes that a word matters if it shows up time and time again. But Voyant does not explain the connection as it leaves all interpretation up to the reader. Chat GPT treats meaning as an explanation. Instead of catching repeated words and phrases, it produced an argument about ethos and pathos, built from patterns it already knows about Poe and the short story. Voyant gives me evidence without a meaning, while GPT gives me a meaning with a lack of evidence. To really understand this short story, I recommend reading it yourself, looking at the numbers Voyant generates, and reading Chat GPT’s conclusion of the story.
